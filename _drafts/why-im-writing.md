Puerto Rico is one of the most misunderstood places to invest in the United States. Most of what gets written about it focuses on tax incentives. Far less covers what it takes to actually put capital to work here: underwriting local businesses, structuring loans that hold up, and running a fund with the discipline investors expect.

<!--more-->

That's what I want to write about.

## What you'll find here

- **Private credit.** How deals are structured, what protects principal, and the lessons that only show up when a loan goes sideways.
- **Fund finance and operations.** Capital calls, NAV, investor reporting and the operating habits that separate well-run funds from the rest.
- **Puerto Rico's capital markets.** Act 60, the local lending landscape, and where I see real opportunity.

## Who it's for

Founders and operators raising capital on the island, finance professionals thinking about moving here, and anyone curious about how private capital actually works.

## A note on what this isn't

These are my own views, not my employer's. I won't discuss specific funds, investments or returns, and nothing here is investment or tax advice.
